Who We Are

Rooted in Kilwa,
Growing for Tanzania

TAMCO is a community-rooted NGO based in Kilwa District, Lindi Region, registered under the NGO Act No. 24 of 2002 (No. 8442) — operating across Tanzania to link conservation with community development.

🌿
About TAMCO
TAMCO was founded to address the dual challenges of environmental degradation and rural poverty by linking conservation with community development across Tanzania.
🎯
Our Mission
To enhance sustainable livelihoods and prevent biodiversity loss by advocating for responsible land use through community outreach and research on agroforestry practices and marine ecosystems.
🌍
Our Vision
A thriving community empowered through sustainable agroforestry and marine conservation practices that promote resilient ecosystems for future generations.
What We Do

Our Programs

Three integrated pillars driving sustainable change across Tanzania's landscapes and coastlines.

View All Programs
Smart Agriculture

Smart Agriculture

Introducing climate-smart farming practices that boost yields while conserving soil, water, and biodiversity across Tanzanian smallholder communities.

  • Drought-resistant crop varieties
  • Soil health & composting training
  • Water harvesting systems
  • Digital farmer extension services
Learn More
Marine Conservation

Mangrove Restoration

Rehabilitating Tanzania's degraded mangrove ecosystems through community-led planting campaigns and coastal fisheries protection programs.

  • Community nursery establishment
  • Coastal erosion prevention
  • Marine biodiversity monitoring
  • Blue carbon sequestration
Learn More
Agroforestry

Agroforestry & Forest Conservation

Integrating indigenous trees with food crops to restore degraded lands, sequester carbon, and provide sustainable timber and non-timber forest products.

  • Native species propagation
  • Farmer-managed natural regeneration
  • Forest carbon projects (REDD+)
  • Beekeeping & forest products
Learn More
Livelihood Support

Livelihood Support

Providing training, microfinance and market access to help communities build sustainable income streams from conservation activities.

  • Microfinance & savings groups
  • Market linkages for conservation products
  • Vocational skills training
  • Women & youth income programmes
Learn More
